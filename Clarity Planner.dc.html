Today, light
Glass is limited to the tab bar, the add button, the timer accessory, the toolbar capsule and the timer sheet. Rings, lists and the timeline sit on near-solid white surfaces over the green and gold wash.
Judgement calls
Timer pill = tab bar bottom accessory. This is the system slot Music uses for Now Playing. When you scroll down, the tab bar shrinks to the active tab and the pill moves into the gap beside it.
Add button is a separate tinted-glass circle to the right of the tab bar, in the slot Apple uses for the Search tab.
Toolbar actions share one glass capsule (calendar and profile), as iOS 26/27 toolbars do.
Weekday as an eyebrow above the date, which keeps the Large Title short, like the Fitness and Health summary pages.
Timer sheet floats inset by 8pt with a 47pt radius (55pt device corner minus 8), so its corners sit concentric with the screen. It scales up from the pill to suggest the morph.
Checklist is an inset grouped list. The tick colour and "Done at" time both use primary green.
